About Promo Radar NG

Promo Radar NG is a free guide to discounts from Nigerian online shops, in one place.

What we do

Shops in Nigeria run sales all the time, and it is hard to keep track of which discounts are real. Promo Radar NG lists deals from shops such as Jumia and Konga, and from other Nigerian retailers, so you can compare them quickly. Each deal shows the current price, the old price, and how much you save.

When you tap a deal, you go straight to the shop's own website to buy. We do not take payment, hold your money, or sell your order.

How deals are checked

  • The deal list is refreshed automatically about every four hours.
  • We skip deals where the "old price" looks inflated, so a small discount is not dressed up as a big one.
  • We only list deals with a meaningful discount, and we point to the shop's own page for the price.

Prices change quickly. Always check the price, delivery fee and stock on the shop's site before you pay.

How this site earns money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the shop or a partner may pay us a small commission. It does not cost you extra. Some deals are paid placements and are marked "Sponsored". Read our affiliate disclosure for details.

Who runs it

Promo Radar NG is run by one person who checks the site and keeps the deal list running. We are not owned by or linked to any of the shops listed.
