Affordable Hotels in Lagos and Abuja: Where to Stay and How to Save

By Udochukwu · Promo Radar NG · 19 October 2026

Whether you're flying home for a wedding, a business meeting or Detty December, where you stay in Lagos or Abuja affects your budget, your safety and how much time you lose in traffic. Here's how to pick the right area and avoid paying too much.

Lagos: pick your area by your plans

Lagos traffic can turn a 10 km trip into two hours. Stay close to where you'll spend most of your time.

Abuja's airport is about 40 minutes from the city centre, so factor in transport costs if you have an early flight.

Hotel or short-let apartment?

Hotels usually include security, steady power and a front desk, which helps for short or business trips. Short-let apartments can be better value for families or longer stays, with a kitchen and more space, but quality varies a lot.

What to check before you book

  • Power: Ask whether there's 24-hour electricity from a generator or inverter, and if it's included
  • Water: Confirm constant running water, especially in short-lets
  • Security: Gated estate, guards, and safe parking if you're driving
  • Wi-Fi: Check reviews for real speeds if you need to work
  • Total cost: Some places add service charge, VAT or a caution fee on top of the listed price

Avoid fake listings

Short-let scams are common, especially around December. Fraudsters copy photos from real apartments and ask for payment to a personal account.

Stay safe: Book through well-known platforms or the hotel's official website where possible. Be very careful paying a "caution fee" or full payment to a personal bank account for a place you haven't verified. Ask for a live video call of the apartment if you're unsure.

Ways to pay less

  • Compare the same hotel on two or three booking sites and its own website
  • Book early for December, when Lagos prices can rise sharply
  • Midweek stays in Abuja are often busy with business travellers, while weekends can be cheaper
  • For longer stays, ask the hotel directly for a weekly or monthly rate
