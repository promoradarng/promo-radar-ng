Cheap Flights from Manchester and Birmingham to Lagos: Routes, Timing and Traps to Avoid

By Udochukwu · Promo Radar NG · 22 October 2026

A huge part of the Nigerian community in the UK lives far from London — in Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Sheffield, Coventry and beyond. Most "cheap flights to Lagos" advice assumes you're starting at Heathrow or Gatwick. If you're not, the cheapest way home can look very different.

1. There's no nonstop flight — so pick your connection wisely

As of October 2026, no airline flies nonstop from Manchester or Birmingham to Lagos. Every trip involves at least one connection. That's not bad news: one-stop fares from regional airports are often cheaper than flying direct from London, once you add the train or coach down south.

Popular one-stop options from Manchester (MAN) and Birmingham (BHX) include:

  • Amsterdam — KLM
  • Paris — Air France
  • Istanbul — Turkish Airlines
  • Doha or Dubai — Qatar Airways, Emirates
  • Casablanca — Royal Air Maroc
  • London — a domestic hop or train, then British Airways, Virgin Atlantic or Air Peace
Check transit rules: Some connection countries require a transit visa for Nigerian passport holders, even airside. If you travel on a Nigerian passport, confirm with the airline before you pay. British passport holders usually have fewer restrictions.

2. Compare "fly from home" with "go to London first"

London has the only nonstop flights to Lagos — British Airways and Virgin Atlantic from Heathrow, and Air Peace from Gatwick. Sometimes a cheap London fare beats every regional option. Do the full sum:

OptionWhat to add up
Fly from MAN or BHX with one stopFare only — usually the simplest
Train or coach to London, then nonstopFare + rail/coach ticket + possibly a night's hotel
Connecting flight via London on one ticketFare only — bags checked through

Advance rail tickets and coaches (National Express, Megabus) can make the London option cheap — but leave plenty of buffer. A late train won't be the airline's problem.

3. Beware the "cheap" self-transfer

Comparison sites sometimes show very low fares that combine a budget airline (for example Ryanair to a European city) with a separate flight to Lagos. These are self-transfer trips on two tickets. If the first flight is late and you miss the second, you usually lose that ticket. You also have to collect and re-check your bags, which may need a visa for that country.

Rule of thumb: If you're carrying heavy bags or travelling with children, pay a little more for one ticket where bags go all the way to Lagos.

5. Baggage is where Nigerians lose money

Trips home usually mean two full suitcases. Allowances differ a lot between airlines, and the cheapest "light" fares may include only one checked bag — or none.

  • Compare fares with your real baggage, not the headline price
  • Gulf and Turkish routes are often generous with checked bags — check your exact fare type
  • Buying an extra bag online before you fly is almost always cheaper than paying at the airport
  • Weigh bags at home; overweight fees at check-in are steep

6. Booking tips that actually work

  • Search both MAN and BHX — and Leeds Bradford or East Midlands if they're near you
  • Use the month view and set price alerts for MAN–LOS and BHX–LOS
  • Midweek departures (Tuesday–Thursday) are often cheaper
  • Book 2–3 months ahead for normal travel, and by summer for December
  • Check the airline's own site before booking through an agent — sometimes it's cheaper or easier to change

Quick checklist

  • ✅ Compare one-stop from your local airport with London nonstops
  • ✅ Count rail or coach costs if you go via London
  • ✅ Avoid self-transfer tickets when carrying heavy bags
  • ✅ Check transit visa rules for your passport
  • ✅ Compare fares with your real baggage included
