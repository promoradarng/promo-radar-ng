Privacy policy

This page explains what information Promo Radar NG collects when you visit promoradarng.com.ng, and why.

You don't need an account

You can use the site without signing up or giving us your name, phone number or email address.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-FJXBL4F648) to count visits and see which deals and pages people look at. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your browser type, approximate location (country or city level) and the pages you visit. We also record when you tap a deal, so we know which deals are popular. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Affiliate links and partner scripts

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, the shop or affiliate network may set its own cookie to track the purchase, so they can pay us commission. We also load a partner script from Travelpayouts on the site. These third parties have their own privacy policies, which we recommend reading.

Favourites

If you tap the heart on a deal, we save that choice in your browser's local storage so it stays there next time. This is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

Advertising

If we show Google AdSense ads in future, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your past visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, email promoradarng@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the bottom shows when it last changed.
